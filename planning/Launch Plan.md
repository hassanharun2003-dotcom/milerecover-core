# MileRecover Launch Plan

**Status:** Planned — launch pricing locked (DEC-018–DEC-024)  
**Target Launch:** Q4 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Owner:** Product / Growth

---

## Launch Thesis

Launch MileRecover as **the most trusted protection system for people who drive for work** — with **bounded recovery at launch**, honest gaps, and proof you can defend.

**Tagline:** Every work mile accounted for.

---

## Launch Personas

Single app; personalized language and defaults (DEC-008):

- Delivery and rideshare drivers
- Realtors
- Contractors and home-service professionals
- Healthcare and mobile-care workers
- Sales professionals
- **Employees receiving reimbursement**
- Business owners
- Drivers with multiple jobs

## Founding Member Pro (DEC-019)

- **Pro tier** at $59.99/year
- Approved beta participants and early launch users

## Messaging Framework

### Primary
**Every work mile accounted for — with proof.**

### Recovery-first (DEC-021)
- We find gaps — you confirm every mile
- Calendar, imports, and edge repair — never silent backfill
- Recovery is why MileRecover exists

### Estimated value (DEC-020)
- Estimated deduction / reimbursement / potential mileage value
- Visible by default after onboarding; user can hide
- Never guaranteed savings or tax outcomes

### Quiet hours (DEC-023)
- Default 9 PM – 7 AM — review notifications wait
- Critical tracking-health only when delay risks lost records

### Avoid
- Guaranteed savings / tax refund claims
- "AI logs everything"
- Surveillance framing
