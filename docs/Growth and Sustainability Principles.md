# MileRecover Growth and Sustainability Principles

**Status:** Foundational — Commercial governance  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Owner:** Founding Team / Product  
**Authority:** Subordinate to [Company Constitution.md](./Company%20Constitution.md), [04 Trust Rules.md](./04%20Trust%20Rules.md), [Product DNA.md](./Product%20DNA.md); governs acquisition, conversion, retention, reviews, referrals, and pricing validation

**Not:** A revenue forecast, growth-hack playbook, or substitute for the [Product Requirements Document.md](./Product%20Requirements%20Document.md).

---

## 1. Purpose

MileRecover’s commercial goal is to:

- **Win users through trust and superior product value** — protection, honest gaps, bounded recovery, defensible proof
- **Build a financially sustainable company** that can operate and improve without compromising users
- **Avoid growth at the expense of** accuracy, privacy, or user confidence
- **Establish foundations** capable of supporting large scale **without treating revenue targets as the product mission**

Growth decisions must pass the [Founding Commercial Principle](#2-founding-commercial-principle) and [Sustainable Growth Hierarchy](#3-sustainable-growth-hierarchy).

---

## 2. Founding Commercial Principle

> **MileRecover does not maximize short-term revenue at the expense of user trust. We build a financially sustainable business by creating lasting value that users willingly pay for.**

### Implications

| Domain | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Ship recurring protection value before monetization experiments; never paywall honesty |
| **Pricing** | Locked launch prices ([09 Pricing.md](./09%20Pricing.md), DEC-018); changes require founder decision + Decision Log (DEC-029) |
| **Free tier** | Genuinely useful — records, manual trips, basic export, health visibility; bounded auto capture for cost control |
| **Paywalls** | Value-first moments only ([§5](#5-value-before-monetization)); no blocking existing records |
| **Retention** | Product habit + trust, not fear, streaks, or export hostage-taking |
| **Support** | Believe user experience; never invent records to close tickets |
| **Infrastructure** | Scale costs consciously; prefer deterministic processing over unnecessary AI spend |
| **Marketing** | Truthful outcomes; no guaranteed tax savings; no fabricated recovery examples |
| **Refunds** | Platform policy; no guilt flows tied to data hostage |
| **Cancellation** | Platform-managed; export reminder; downgrade retains stored data + basic CSV |
| **Data after downgrade** | User retains access to own records; Free limits apply to **new** recurring capabilities only |

Recorded as **DEC-025**.

## 3. Sustainable Growth Hierarchy

When commercial and product priorities conflict, resolve in this order:

| Rank | Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Truth and data integrity** | Never invent mileage; preserve evidence; honest gaps |
| 2 | **User safety and privacy** | No data sale; minimization; personal-route controls |
| 3 | **Reliable recurring value** | Tracking, Protection Health, review, proof that works weekly |
| 4 | **User trust and retention** | Calm UX; transparent billing; support quality |
| 5 | **Financial sustainability** | Unit economics discipline; avoid scaling broken channels |
| 6 | **Responsible acquisition** | Educate and demonstrate; no fear or fabrication |
| 7 | **Revenue optimization** | A/B tests within trust bounds; no dark patterns |
| 8 | **Download volume and vanity metrics** | Context only — never optimize alone |

**Lower ranks may never override higher ranks.**

Aligns with [Product DNA §3](./Product%20DNA.md#3-product-decision-hierarchy) for product tradeoffs; this hierarchy adds **commercial** specificity.

## 4. User Acquisition Principles

### Approved foundations

- **Competitor import + gap preview** (Free 7-day; honest migration)
- **Value before purchase** — capture, review, health visibility on Free
- **Search intent** — missed/forgotten mileage, reimbursement, audit-ready logs (metadata only; no false claims)
- **Professional referrals** — CPAs, bookkeepers who value defensible exports
- **Accountant / bookkeeper education** — Proof Score, gap disclosure, source labeling
- **Creator demonstrations** — truthful product outcomes only; disclose limitations
- **User referrals after genuine success** — separate from review prompts ([§8](#8-referral-principles))
- **App Store discovery** — accurate screenshots, four-tab calm UX
- **Educational content** — IRS log requirements, protection vs maximization
- **Rescue products** — honest scoped recovery ($14.99 / $29.99); not subscription trickery

### Prohibited acquisition behavior

- Fabricated recovered-value examples presented as real user outcomes
- Fear-based claims without evidence ("audit coming — act now")
- Misleading competitor comparisons or cherry-picked screenshots
- Fake scarcity or countdown timers (DEC-014)
- Bought or incentivized App Store reviews
- Spam, cold outreach, or permission-less contact harvesting
- Dark patterns (hidden close, pre-checked expensive tier, confusion)
- Unsubstantiated tax guarantees or "IRS approved" language (DEC-009)
- Claims that MileRecover detects **every** trip under **every** condition

## 5. Value Before Monetization

### Approved paywall moments

- After user sees **credible value** from tracking or recovery (confirmed trip, gap surfaced honestly)
- When a **Free limit is actually reached** (40 auto trips/month, 1 missing-trip scan/month, 7-day import preview elapsed)
- When user **deliberately selects** a paid capability (PDF export, Proof Score, calendar recovery, Pro workflow)
- After **competitor-import preview** demonstrates relevant gaps
- When **generating export** requiring Plus/Pro tier
- When selecting **Pro workflow** (businesses, reimbursement CSV, Proof Package, sharing)

### Prohibited paywall moments

- Before user understands product (onboarding, first launch)
- During **OS permission request** or permission education
- During **tracking failure remediation** (Protection Health fix flows)
- During **account deletion** or data export for deletion prep
- During **support escalation** or open trust incident
- Immediately after an **error** or crash
- By **blocking access to existing user records**

### Calm paywall requirements

- Tier comparison sheet: clear feature list, price, billing period, renewal terms
- **Continue Free** always visible
- No fake urgency; Founding Member terms explicit (DEC-019)
- Entitlement state visible in Profile (current tier, renewal date, restore purchases)
- Rescue IAP scope stated before purchase (3 months / 1 tax year)

Cross-reference: [Product Requirements Document.md §16](./Product%20Requirements%20Document.md).

## 6. Retention Principles

### Retention from recurring product value

- Reliable automatic capture (within tier limits)
- **Protection Health** honesty when degraded
- **Exception-based weekly review** — not every drive
- **Missing-trip detection** with user confirmation
- **Monthly completeness** and gap visibility
- **Proof** and exports that reduce employer/CPA friction
- **Multi-job / multi-vehicle continuity** (Plus+)
- **Employee reimbursement workflow** (Pro)

### Retention must not depend on

- Artificial streak anxiety or gamification
- Excessive notifications (DEC-011, DEC-023)
- Hidden cancellation paths
- Loss of access to **existing** records
- Manipulative countdowns or "your miles expire" fiction
- Shame, fear, or deduction-maximizer copy
- Making export intentionally difficult after user paid or while on Free basic CSV rights

## 7. App Store Review Request Strategy

**Authoritative policy — DEC-026.** Implementation: [Product Requirements Document.md](./Product%20Requirements%20Document.md); experience: [Experience Bible.md](../design/Experience%20Bible.md).

### Eligibility (all required)

1. User experienced a **verified positive value event** (see candidates below)
2. **No unresolved critical tracking problem** (Protection Health ≠ Action needed for permission/engine failure)
3. **No active crisis:** payment dispute, cancellation in progress, refund, permission remediation flow, deletion, or open support trust ticket
4. User has **not recently dismissed** a review request (cooldown enforced)
5. **Platform frequency rules** respected (StoreKit / Play In-App Review quotas)
6. App **continues safely** if OS does not show the dialog

### Candidate positive value events (not automatic)

Conservative eligibility engine — **at most one prompt attempt per qualifying window**; product may require **two independent signals** before eligibility:

| Event | Notes |
|---|---|
| First **user-confirmed recovered** trip | After accept, not at suggestion |
| Successful **weekly review completion** with ≥3 real trips reviewed | Not first session |
| **Meaningful Proof Score improvement** on a period (e.g., +10 pts after user actions) | Plus+ |
| Successful **report or reimbursement export** shared or saved | Export completed, not paywall |
| **Completed month** with no unresolved gaps (user-confirmed or accounted days) | Honest completeness |
| Recovery that **materially improved completeness** | User-initiated dismiss resets cooldown only |

### Prohibited review moments

Onboarding · First launch · Before first captured trip · Immediately after subscribe · Immediately after paywall · After permission denial · After tracking failure · After error · During cancellation · During refund discussion · Immediately after support closes complaint · After data deletion · **Repeated custom prompts** after user dismissed · Any moment coupling review to referral reward

### Implementation rules

- Use **native** `SKStoreReviewController` / Play In-App Review API only — no fake system dialogs
- **No reward** for reviews (DEC-026)
- **No rating gating** (happy → public, unhappy → private)
- **No coupling** review requests to referral incentives
- **Analytics:** log `review_prompt_eligible`, `review_prompt_attempted`, `review_prompt_dismissed_cooldown` — **never** store user’s star rating (private to Apple/Google)
- **Frequency:** max **1 attempt per 120 days** per user after any attempt; **90-day cooldown** after dismiss; stricter if OS denies display
- **Acceptance:** QA verifies prohibited moments never trigger; eligible moments respect cooldown

## 8. Referral Principles

Referrals are **separate** from review prompts.

### Requirements

- Request only after **meaningful value** (e.g., successful export month — not same session as review prompt)
- **Clear reward terms** if incentive exists (e.g., credit month) — no "review us for reward"
- **Abuse protection** — caps, fraud checks, self-referral block
- **No contact access** without deliberate share-sheet action
- **No automatic messaging** to contacts
- **No misleading claims** about referral bonus or tax outcomes
- **No pressure** immediately after recovered-value event
- User may **dismiss permanently** (Profile → Referrals off)

## 9. Pricing and Validation Principles

### Locked launch prices (DEC-018 — do not change without founder decision)

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Free | $0 |
| Plus | $6.99/month · $49.99/year |
| Pro | $10.99/month · $79.99/year |
| Mileage Rescue | $14.99 one-time |
| Full-Year Rescue | $29.99 one-time |
| Founding Member Pro | $59.99/year (eligible early users, DEC-019) |

### Governance (DEC-029)

- Prices are **active launch decisions**
- Beta may test **perceived value** and willingness to pay (Van Westendorp, interviews)
- Research is **informational** — does not automatically change prices
- Any price change → **formal founder decision** + Decision Log entry
- Existing subscriber terms remain **transparent**; Founding Member grandfathering per DEC-019
- **No fake discounts** — displayed savings must match monthly×12 vs annual math
- Annual equivalent pricing shown clearly ($/month equivalent optional, not misleading)
- Store tax and platform fee disclosure where required

## 10. Free-Tier Sustainability

Free exists to **demonstrate honest protection**, not to trick users into paid.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Records | Free users **retain** stored trips |
| Manual trips | **Unlimited** |
| Auto capture | **40 trips/month** (locked) |
| Health | Basic **Protection Health** visibility |
| Export | **Basic CSV** of confirmed trips |
| Cost control | Limits bound recurring infra (auto processing, scans, import preview) |
| Honest demo | Full review, reject, gap, no-driving flows on Free |
| Not unusable | Free must not be broken intentionally to force upgrade |
| Paid justification | Unlimited auto, Proof Score, PDF, calendar recovery, multi-vehicle — ongoing cost + depth |

## 11. Unit-Economics Discipline

**Principles only — no fabricated benchmarks.**

- Measure **CAC by channel** when spending begins
- Measure **retention by plan and persona**
- Measure **support cost per active user** and per trust incident
- Measure variable cost drivers: mapping/geocoding, storage, AI inference, notifications, export compute
- **Prefer deterministic** rules over AI where sufficient (Proof Score v1, gap detection)
- **Avoid paid acquisition** until retention and contribution economics support it
- **Do not scale** a channel with negative contribution margin
- **Annual cash collection ≠ monthly earned revenue** — recognize appropriately in finance reporting
- Include **refunds, store fees, taxes, chargebacks** in contribution analysis
- Distinguish **gross billings, recognized revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, operating profit**

## 13. Founder Decision Filter

Before major product or commercial changes, answer:

1. Does this improve or preserve **truth**?
2. Does this increase **user trust**?
3. Does this provide **recurring user value**?
4. Is it **simple and understandable**?
5. Is it **financially sustainable**?
6. Can its claims be **proven**?
7. Could it pressure a **vulnerable or frustrated** user?
8. Would we make this if **downloads were not publicly visible**?
9. Does it preserve user **access to their own records**?
10. Does it create **long-term advantage**, not temporary extraction?

All ten should be "yes" on 1–4 and 9; "no" on 7; "yes" on 8 and 10 for approval.

## 14. Explicit Anti-Patterns

- Growth at all costs
- Revenue as the product mission
- Paywalls before value without justified product reason
- Review manipulation or incentivized ratings
- Notification engagement bait
- Fake urgency
- Feature inflation beyond [Feature Admission Test](./Product%20DNA.md#10-feature-admission-test)
- Selling location data
- Hiding tracking degradation
- Inflating estimated value (DEC-009)
- Treating AI output as evidence
- Scaling paid acquisition before retention proof
- Tiers manufactured solely to confuse
- Cancellation friction as retention
- Coupling referrals to reviews
