# MileRecover Export Disclaimer

**Status:** Draft — Legal review required  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Owner:** Legal / Product

---

## Purpose

This text appears on exported mileage reports and in export preview UI. It sets honest expectations: MileRecover is a **record-keeping tool**, not tax advice.

Referenced in [Launch Plan.md](../planning/Launch%20Plan.md) pre-launch checklist.

---

## User-Facing Disclaimer (PDF footer)

```
Generated by MileRecover on [date] · [device/sync status]

This mileage log reflects trips you confirmed in MileRecover. Auto-detected trips
are included only after your review unless you enabled automatic confirmation above
your chosen Proof Score threshold.

MileRecover does not provide tax, legal, or accounting advice. Business mileage
deductibility depends on your facts and circumstances. Consult a qualified tax
professional before filing.

Pending review trips [included/excluded]. Unaccounted days: [count/list if any].
AI-assisted classifications are marked in the export metadata.

Every work mile accounted for — never invent mileage.
```

---

## In-App Export Preview Banner

> **Before you export:** This report includes **confirmed business miles** only (unless you choose to include pending trips). Gaps in tracking are shown honestly — MileRecover does not estimate missing mileage. Your CPA can help determine deductibility.

---

## CPA Metadata Appendix (CSV / optional PDF)

Included columns for transparency ([Export Formats.md](../architecture/Export%20Formats.md)):

- `source` — auto, manual, recovered, imported
- `proof_score` — 0–100 at export time
- `user_confirmed_at` — ISO timestamp
- `ai_assisted` — true/false
- `flags` — gap, incomplete, etc.

---

## What We Never Say on Exports

Per [Anti-Principles.md](./Anti-Principles.md):

- "IRS approved" or "audit guaranteed"
- "Estimated savings: $X,XXX"
- "100% complete" when gaps exist
- Unmarked AI-generated purpose text
