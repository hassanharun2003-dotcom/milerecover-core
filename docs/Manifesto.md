# The MileRecover Manifesto

**Audience:** Customers, partners, and anyone asking why we exist  
**Status:** Foundational  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

## You drove. You just couldn't prove it.

If you work for yourself, your car is part of your livelihood. Showings. Job sites. Client visits. Supply runs. The miles add up — and so does the quiet frustration of trying to remember them all.

Most people don't fail at driving for work. They fail at **accounting** for it.

Spreadsheets go empty. Apps get uninstalled. Tracking gets turned off because the battery couldn't take it. And when tax season arrives, the question isn't only *how many miles* — it's *can I stand behind this log?*

That's the problem MileRecover is built to solve.

---

## Every work mile accounted for

Not every mile *guessed*. Not every mile *inflated*. Every **legitimate work mile**, captured with evidence you can review, confirm, and export.

We believe completeness means honesty: if a day has no record, we show the gap. We don't paper over it. We help you recover what's real — from your odometer, your calendar, your memory — without inventing what isn't there.

**Never invent mileage.** That's not a footnote. It's the foundation.

---

## Trust over automation

We've seen what "automatic" mileage tracking looks like in the wild. The errand logged as a business trip. The drive that never happened. The total that looks impressive until your accountant asks questions.

Automation should save you time — not steal your confidence.

MileRecover detects movement and proposes trips. **You** confirm what counts as business. You reject what didn't happen. You see why a trip was flagged and how strong the evidence is.

We'd rather miss a trip you add manually than log one you can't defend.

---

## Calm over anxiety

Tax season creates enough stress. Your mileage log shouldn't make it worse.

We won't flash inflated savings numbers. We won't guilt you into claiming more. We won't hide weak entries behind green checkmarks.

Instead: a clear review queue, a Proof Score that explains defensibility, and exports that tell the truth — including what's pending, what's personal, and what's still unaccounted.

The goal isn't a bigger number on a dashboard. It's **calm** when you hand the file to your CPA.

---

## Built for the road you're actually on

**Offline first.** Dead zones shouldn't mean lost data. Review and export work without signal.

**Battery friendly.** Tracking that kills your phone gets turned off — and then nothing gets recorded. We treat battery life as a feature, not an afterthought.

**Accuracy over features.** We'd rather ship one thing correctly than ten things that merely look competitive.

---

## AI assists. It doesn't decide.

Where AI helps — suggesting a business purpose, reading an odometer photo, flagging something unusual — it works in the open. Labeled. Editable. Optional.

AI does not create your mileage log. **You** do. With evidence. With confirmation. With a record you own.

---

## Fairness, not hacks

MileRecover is for people building real businesses: realtors, contractors, therapists with client visits, independents running real routes. You deserve tools that treat your records like what they are — **financial evidence** — not gamified deduction bait.

We're not here to maximize your mileage on paper. We're here to help you account for the miles you actually drove.

---

## This is MileRecover

**Every work mile accounted for.**

With proof. With control. Without invention.

If that sounds like how you'd want a mileage log to behave — we're building it for you.
