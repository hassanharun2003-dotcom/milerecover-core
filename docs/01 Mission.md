# MileRecover Mission

**Status:** Foundational  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Owner:** Founding Team

---

## Mission Statement

**MileRecover helps self-employed professionals capture, verify, and recover every legitimate work mile—with evidence they can trust.**

We build tools that respect the user's reality: incomplete records, battery constraints, spotty connectivity, and the legitimate fear of getting mileage wrong at tax time.

---

## Why We Exist

The mileage deduction is one of the most commonly claimed—and most commonly challenged—business expenses in the United States. Existing apps optimize for:

- Automatic trip detection (often wrong)
- Maximum logged miles (often inflated)
- Feature parity (rarely audit-ready)

Users don't need more miles logged. They need **every work mile accounted for** without **invented mileage**.

---

## Who We Serve

Our primary users are people whose income depends on driving for work and who lack employer-provided mileage systems:

- Independent contractors and 1099 workers
- Real estate agents and property managers
- Gig economy drivers (non-platform-captive)
- Field service professionals
- Small business owners with mixed personal/business use

Secondary stakeholders: CPAs, bookkeepers, and tax preparers who need clean exports—not another app their clients can't explain.

See [05 User Personas.md](./05%20User%20Personas.md) for full profiles.

## How We Fulfill the Mission

| Capability | Mission Alignment |
|---|---|
| Native Tracking Engine | Captures movement with minimal battery impact |
| Recovery Engine | Helps reconstruct defensible logs from partial data |
| Proof Score | Surfaces confidence; never hides uncertainty |
| Offline-first architecture | Works where work actually happens |
| AI-assisted classification | Proposes labels; user confirms; evidence preserved |

---

## Operating Principles

Every team member operates under MileRecover philosophy:

- **Trust over automation** — propose, don't presume
- **Accuracy over features** — ship proof before polish
- **Never invent mileage** — gaps stay gaps until evidence exists
- **Battery friendly** — if tracking drains the phone, tracking stops
- **Offline first** — sync is async; capture is immediate
- **AI assists but never replaces evidence** — models suggest; sensors and users decide

---

## What Success Looks Like (Mission Level)

- Users export mileage logs their CPA accepts without rework
- Proof Scores correlate with real audit outcomes
- Users describe MileRecover as "the app that doesn't lie to me"
- Zero marketing claims we cannot substantiate with product behavior

---

## What We Say No To

- Auto-logging trips below confidence thresholds
- Backfilling historical mileage without source data
- Features that increase logged miles but decrease defensibility
- Dark patterns that pressure users to "claim more"
