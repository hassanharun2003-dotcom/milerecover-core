# MileRecover Vision

**Status:** Foundational  
**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Owner:** Founding Team

---

## The Future We Are Building

MileRecover exists to solve a quiet crisis: millions of self-employed professionals, gig workers, and small business owners lose legitimate tax deductions every year—not because they didn't drive, but because they couldn't prove it.

We envision a world where **every work mile is accounted for**—automatically, accurately, and with evidence a user would trust in an audit.

Not another mileage app that guesses. Not another dashboard that optimizes for engagement. A **recovery system** that treats mileage like financial records: precise, auditable, and defensible.

---

## What MileRecover Becomes

In five years, MileRecover is the **operating system for work-related travel evidence**:

- The default tool for contractors, real estate agents, delivery drivers, field technicians, and anyone whose income depends on wheels on the road
- The brand synonymous with **audit-ready mileage**—not "smart tracking"
- A platform where AI **assists classification and review** but **never replaces evidence**
- A company trusted by accountants, tax professionals, and users who have been burned by inflated or invented logs

## North Star Statement

> **Every work mile accounted for—with proof.**

If a user cannot explain, review, and export how a mile was recorded, we did not account for it.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

All product, engineering, and business decisions flow through these principles:

1. **Every work mile accounted for** — completeness without fabrication
2. **Never invent mileage** — if we didn't detect it, we don't log it
3. **Trust over automation** — the user remains the authority; automation proposes
4. **Accuracy over features** — we ship proof, not novelty
5. **Battery friendly** — sustainable tracking beats perfect tracking
6. **Offline first** — the road has dead zones; our product must not
7. **AI assists but never replaces evidence** — intelligence supports judgment, not substitutes it

---

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Evidence-First Tracking
Every logged mile links to sensor data, timestamps, and user confirmation where required. The **Native Tracking Engine** captures; the **Recovery Engine** reconciles; the **Proof Score** quantifies defensibility.

### 2. Radical Transparency
Users see exactly what was detected, what was inferred, and what was excluded. No black boxes. No silent auto-logging of ambiguous movement.

### 3. Professional-Grade Export
IRS-ready reports, CPA-friendly formats, and audit trails that stand up to scrutiny—not PDFs designed for App Store screenshots.

### 4. Human-Centered Recovery
Many users arrive with incomplete historical records. MileRecover helps **recover** defensible mileage from partial evidence—not fabricate missing history.
