## Standard Mileage Rate (2026 Reference)

The IRS standard mileage rate for business use of a vehicle is published annually (typically late year for following tax year).

**Product implication:** MileRecover displays current IRS rate in exports with `tax_year` field. Rate updates via remote config, not app update.

**Research action:** Monitor [IRS standard mileage rates](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates) at release time.

## What IRS Requires in a Mileage Log

Per IRS Publication 463 (*Travel, Gift, and Car Expenses*) and historical audit guidance, contemporaneous records should include:

| Field | Required | MileRecover field |
|---|---|---|
| Date | ✓ | `start_at` / trip date |
| Destination | ✓ | Geocoded end address / user entry |
| Business purpose | ✓ | `purpose` (user-attested) |
| Miles driven | ✓ | `distance_meters` converted |
| Odometer (optional but strong) | Recommended | Odometer capture flow |

## Business vs. Personal

**IRS principle:** Commuting from home to regular workplace is generally **not** deductible. Travel between work locations, to clients, or for business errands **may** be.

**Product implication:**
- MileRecover provides classification tools, not tax advice
- No auto-assumption that all driving is business
- Educational copy on commuting vs business (Settings → Learn)
- **Never invent mileage** — user attests business purpose

---

## Contemporaneous Records Standard

IRS prefers records created **at or near time** of travel. Reconstructed records are permitted but may face scrutiny.

**MileRecover alignment:**
- Auto-detected trips timestamped at capture → strong contemporaneous evidence
- Recovery Engine reconstructions flagged as `source: recovered` with lower Proof Score
- Export distinguishes real-time vs recovered entries

This supports honest recovery without equating it to live capture.

## Standard Mileage Rate vs. Actual Expenses

Users choose one method per vehicle per year (with exceptions). MileRecover focuses on **mileage logging** for standard rate method.

**Future (H3):** Actual expense method support (gas, depreciation) — out of MVP scope.

---

## Audit Risk Factors

Documented IRS audit triggers (general industry knowledge):
- Disproportionately high mileage relative to income
- Round numbers throughout log
- Missing purpose fields
- 100% business use claims

**MileRecover design responses:**

| Risk | Product mitigation |
|---|---|
| Round numbers | Preserve GPS precision; no rounding up |
| Missing purpose | Proof Score penalizes; export advisory |
| Inflated totals | **Accuracy over features**; no "maximize" UX |
| 100% business | Personal classification encouraged; stats show split |

---

## Record Retention

IRS generally: retain records **3 years** from filing date (longer in some cases).

**MileRecover default retention:** 7 years (user configurable)
